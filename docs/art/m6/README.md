# M6 ambient life

Three civilian appearances are original code-authored variations of the existing shared `WorldArt.character` body: commuter with cap/backpack, shopper with vest/bag, and visitor with scarf/shoulder bag. They use the existing authored ambient-route motor and remain non-authoritative. The existing Quaternius CC0-derived watcher GLB and hero GLB are unchanged; their source and licence records remain in `assets-source/g2/README.md` and `assets-source/hero/README.md`.

Four pigeons are original low-poly code-authored vertex-coloured meshes, sharing one source geometry and material. Idle drift, peck and brief scatter are presentation only. No external model, texture, audio, asset pack, service or new dependency was used. One additional walker has no shadow caster; birds have no shadow casters or pick targets.

At the fixed camera, this is the selected small ambient population. Scooter riders and visual traffic were not added because their moving silhouettes and route work offered less benefit than the delivered pedestrian and bird motion.
